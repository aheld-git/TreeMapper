TreeMapper · architecture of the repository at one commit

A drone that maps an orchard from stereo video, then flies tree to tree and sprays each one

TreeMapper is the perception stage. It turns tree boxes from a detector, stereo disparity and the drone's pose into a map of trees (stable id, ground outline, centre, canopy height, sprayed flag). A corridor navigator flies on that map. The repo also holds an Isaac Sim orchard that closes the loop, and the tools that score each mission against ground truth.

One unit of work: one frame through cand_obj

Every detection message that arrives becomes one frame. These are the steps from CandObj::process (cand_obj.cpp:219-276), in order. Line numbers are in cand_obj.cpp unless named.

  1. Pair the inputs

    The node matches each detection set with the disparity grid of the same stamp, else the nearest within 20 ms, else none. It looks up the drone pose by stamp and clamps frame time to be monotonic.

    Output: FrameInput (t, stamp, pose, phase, hop_target, boxes, disparity grid)

    ros/treemapper_ros/src/input_sync.cpp: FrameSync; treemapper_node.cpp: processFrame

  2. Phase policy

    The mission phase decides whether the mapper is awake, may create or merge trees, and whether non-target trees are frozen. A lock after PLANNING stays until the next SCAN.

    Output: PhasePolicy

    cand_obj.cpp: policyFor :199-217

  3. Early outs and frame quality

    Sleeping phases, a lost pose or an empty disparity skip the frame and bump a counter. Otherwise the rotation rate from consecutive poses gives a quality weight.

    Output: q, 0 to 1

    cand_obj.cpp: process :232-266, frameQuality :176

  4. Gate points and bin the canopy model

    Disparity is back-projected to world points, gated by range and height above the drone, weighted by distance and tilt, and binned on a 0.4 m world lattice. A frame with a noise burst is dropped.

    Output: median z per cell, valid flag

    cand_obj.cpp: extractCrowns :288-373

  5. Ground plane and canopy cells

    A plane is fitted to the lowest 40 % of cells. Cells are smoothed 3x3 and called canopy when they rise at least min_above_ground over the plane.

    Output: cell state 0 unknown, 1 ground, 2 canopy

    cand_obj.cpp: extractCrowns :374-452

  6. Watershed into crowns

    Steepest ascent with prominence merging splits the canopy into crowns. A crown counts only if its top lies inside a detection box. Crowns whose peak is at the image edge are flagged truncated.

    Output: Crown list (cells, peak, boxed, truncated)

    cand_obj.cpp: extractCrowns :453-590

  7. Match or seed an instance

    Each crown is matched to a tree instance by world geometry, with no 2D tracks. An unmatched crown seeds a tentative instance when the policy allows creation.

    Output: cell owner map

    cand_obj.cpp: findInstanceForCrown :595-642, integrate :1022-1038

  8. Evidence and latching

    Each instance keeps per-cell counts. A cell latches for good after 5 quality-weighted canopy frames at 75 % or more of the frames it was judged.

    Output: latched cells, observation-weighted support

    cand_obj.cpp: updateEvidence :674-736

  9. Hull, centre, height

    The outline is the convex hull of latched cells, so it only grows. Height is the 0.99 quantile of the fused surface. The centre is the centroid of well-supported cells, held within 1.0 m of the outline (D66). Both move at a bounded rate.

    Output: hull, centre, height

    cand_obj.cpp: refreshHull :738, refreshCentreSupport :763, refreshHeight :812, stepPublished :832

  10. Merge and read out

    Fragments of one tree merge when the policy allows. Only instances with at least 3 outline vertices and a finite height are published.

    Output: vector of TreeEntry, sorted by id

    cand_obj.cpp: mergeInstances :895, publishedTrees :1110

Frame work is timed in two stages, chm (steps 4 to 6) and instances (steps 7 to 10). The ROS node adds node_frame_total and node_disparity_sample.

Second flow: one tree visit in the navigator

Navigator::step is deterministic and has no ROS in it. It takes time, pose, an optional map snapshot, clearance and requests, and returns a setpoint, a nozzle flag, a phase string, a target id and an optional request to mark a tree sprayed (navigator.hpp:44-64).

  1. Lock the corridor

    After a corridor has been scanned out and has settled, the tree list is assigned and ordered from exit back to entry (axial sort plus 2-opt). In field_first mode one lock covers all corridors.

    State: LOCK, phase PLANNING

    navigator.cpp: startLock :353-380, orderStops

  2. Glide to the tree

    The path follows the canopy. If a straight segment is too close to a neighbour it becomes up, over and down. The clearance guard climbs when the nadir reading drops below 1.5 m and gives up after 3 climbs.

    State: GLIDE, phase HOPPING

    navigator.cpp: stepGlide :252-275; navigator_safety.cpp: guardUpdate :75-135; glide.cpp

  3. Dose

    The drone holds at the spray point. The nozzle opens only within 0.3 m of it. Dose time is 0.3 s per m² of outline, clamped to 2 to 6 s, and valid at 80 % open time.

    State: DOSE, phase SPRAYING, nozzle on

    navigator.cpp: stepDose :277-313; spray.cpp

  4. Mark the tree sprayed

    The node calls the TreeMapper service set_tree_sprayed from an async worker. A silent service is retried 3 times at 2 s, then the navigator holds.

    Effect: sprayed flag set, tree frozen in the map

    navigator_safety.cpp: pollMark :143-181; corridor_nav_node.cpp :425-451

  5. Advance

    The next target comes from the corridor. When targets run out the navigator builds a cleanup list of late trees and of trees parked for an implausible height that are now plausible. A retry is dropped if it sits within 1.5 m of a sprayed tree.

    List: cleanup, 2 passes

    navigator.cpp: advance :457-535, collectImplausibleRetries :600-627, nearSprayed :629-634

  6. Return and land

    When nothing is left the drone flies home at scan altitude and lands. Repeated hold timeouts or a planning failure go to ABORT, which shows ERROR for one tick and then returns.

    States: RETURN, LAND, DONE

    navigator.cpp: startReturn :404-413; navigator_safety.cpp: startAbort :267-277

Strategies: corridor locks after every corridor and sprays it before moving on. field_first scans all corridors, locks once, then sprays. The spec says field_first exists only to measure what corridor mode saves (corridor-spray-nav-design.md:249-251).

Second flow: one mission run in the simulator

What scripts/sim_mission.sh does for each run (run_one, sim_mission.sh:255-346). Every run gets a fresh Isaac and a fresh tm_sim_ros.

  1. Pre-flight

    Refuses to start if tm_isaac or tm_sim_ros is running or the work drive has under 10 GB free. Builds the five Noetic packages in a dedicated catkin workspace unless --no-build.

    Output: devel/setup.bash

    sim_mission.sh :123-183

  2. Write the run inputs

    Writes run.json, spec.json, field.yaml and corridor_nav.cfg with the chosen strategy.

    Output: run folder

    tools/treemapper_tools/sim_inputs.py; sim_mission.sh :261-266

  3. Start the bridge, then Isaac

    Starts tm_sim_ros bridge and waits up to 90 s for the bridge. Starts tm_isaac, which runs orchard_sim.py and logs to isaac.log.

    Containers: tm_sim_ros, tm_isaac

    sim_mission.sh :270-295

  4. Wait for /clock

    Polls for the first /clock for up to 400 s wall and fails at once if Isaac exits. This is where the Isaac 5.1 crash on the RTX 5080 showed up.

    Gate: /clock received

    sim_mission.sh :299-311; sim_mission_driver.py wait-clock

  5. Launch perception, TreeMapper, navigator and recorder

    sim_closed_loop.launch starts SGBM, the detector, the TreeMapper node, the navigator and rosbag record with an explicit topic allow-list.

    Output: mission.bag (lz4)

    ros/corridor_nav_ros/launch/sim_closed_loop.launch :41-90

  6. Drive the mission

    The driver waits for every input to appear, calls the navigator's start service and logs state changes until DONE, a stall, the timeout or low disk. Exit 0 is DONE, 3 is timeout, 4 startup, 5 disk, 6 clock stalled, 7 navigator gone.

    Output: driver.json

    scripts/sim_mission_driver.py: run :118-239

  7. Stop and score

    Closes the bag, stops the two containers, and runs mission_score on the run folder. With --record-frames it also converts the run to frames.bin and replaces the bag with a filtered copy.

    Output: scorecard.md, mission_score.json

    sim_mission.sh :194-217, :336-345

  8. Compare

    After several runs, mission_score --compare writes compare.md. Exit codes: 0 all passed, 2 a run failed a row, 1 setup failure.

    Output: compare.md

    sim_mission.sh :364-372

Third flow: replay and scoring

The replay path rebuilds the map from a recording without running Isaac, so a change to the mapper can be measured in seconds.

  1. Record only what the mapper reads

    --record-frames records the stride-4 disparity grid (about 64 kB per image instead of about 1 MB), the camera infos and the pose. A run folder is 120 to 400 MB this way.

    Topic: /rec/disparity_grid (DisparityGrid)

    ros/sim_perception/scripts/disparity_grid_node.py

  2. Convert and check

    sim_frames writes frames.bin (format TMF1). The check fails the run if any disparity is malformed, fewer than 80 % of detection frames got a disparity, or over 1 % of stereo images were lost.

    Output: frames.bin + frames.bin.meta.json

    tools/treemapper_tools/sim_frames.py (403 lines); frames.py :46

  3. Replay through a mapper

    treemapper_replay feeds the frames to a registered mapper with an emulated navigator that resolves the recorded targets to its own ids and marks trees sprayed when SPRAYING ends.

    Output: map.jsonl, targets.json, counters.json

    core/apps/replay_main.cpp: usage :80, default system :86, target resolve :58-68

  4. Measure against truth

    replay_metrics reports height error and centre error for each tree at the lock and at each dose, and the A1 to A3 acceptance rows.

    Output: metrics.json

    tools/treemapper_tools/replay_metrics.py

  5. Gate: replay equals the closed loop

    At least 90 % of paired entries must agree within 0.15 m on final height and centre. It passes on all four datasets: 100 % within 0.15 m, largest final height difference 0.0004 m, largest final centre difference 0.0252 m.

    Pinned: results/miss_reduction/baseline/*/metrics.json, sha256 in the doc

    results/miss_reduction.md :75-110, :154-186

Runtime: who runs on which thread

The closed loop is processes and one thread each. Time runs left to right along the data flow. Colour is the process.

Isaac (tm_isaac)bridgeperception nodestreemapper_nodecorridor_navdriver and recorder
  • treemapper_node has one ros::spin() thread, so callbacks and timers run one at a time and the node has no lock of its own. A second thread exists only with pose_source=pose_topic. Every callback is wrapped so an exception is counted and the node keeps running (treemapper_node.cpp:214-239). Disparity queue 20, detections queue 50.
  • corridor_nav pumps one thread with callAvailable(0.05). The tick and a 0.5 s status timer run on it. At most 8 set_tree_sprayed calls are pending in async workers (corridor_nav_node.cpp:425-451, :680-692).
  • Perception runs in rospy receive threads. The SGBM publisher queue of 2 is the documented place where images are lost under load. The detector takes only the newest frame (queue 1).
  • Isaac runs physics at 60 Hz with a camera render every sixth step, headless. It republishes /tf_static and /sim/truth once a second because the bridge's ROS 2 to ROS 1 side is unlatched (orchard_sim.py:269-273).
  • Respawn is off for the TreeMapper and navigator nodes because a restart resets tree ids to 0.

Across runs: what persists and what reads it

TreeMapper keeps state inside a run and nothing between runs. What travels from one run to the next is files.

  1. Within a frame sequence. CandObj keeps every live instance, a monotonic id counter (ids are never reused), the sticky list lock, the post-spray hold and the pose history for the rotation rate (cand_obj.hpp:247-270). Each instance holds a 28 x 28 cell window of evidence, latched cells, the fused heights and the published hull, centre and height (cand_obj.hpp:195-224).
  2. What reads it. Latched cells feed the hull, matching and merging. Observation-weighted support feeds only the centre. Fused heights feed height and the guard that stops a tree seen only with its top outside the image from publishing. Sprayed instances are never updated again.
  3. Within a mission. The navigator keeps the sprayed and attempted sets, the cleanup list, the parked implausible trees and its counters. It learns a tree is sprayed from its own dose and tells TreeMapper through the service.
  4. Between runs. Nothing in memory. Each run starts fresh containers. A run leaves a folder: run.json, spec.json, truth.json, ground_truth.json, deposition.json, mission.bag, driver.json, logs, scorecard.md, mission_score.json, optionally frames.bin.
  5. Carried forward. frames.bin feeds every later replay of the same mission. The pinned baseline metrics.json files (sha256 in miss_reduction.md:167-170) are what a changed mapper is compared against.
  6. Where the files live. Run folders and frames live on the work drive, /data/work in the containers, which is $TM_WORK_HOST on the host. They are not in git.

Test boundary

Ground truth is read only by scoring and test code. A search of the repo for the truth and deposition files found hits only in the places in the left column, and none in core, nav or the node sources. This was a file-name search, not a read of every source.

Reads truth or mocks

  • sim/ writes truth.json, ground_truth.json, deposition.json and publishes /sim/truth, /sim/deposition
  • tools/treemapper_tools/*: score, mission_score, replay_metrics, evaluate, sim_compare_report, synth
  • scripts/sim_mission.sh lists the truth files as run files; score_baseline.sh
  • sim_closed_loop.launch only records the truth topics into the bag. Nothing subscribes.
  • Tests: ctest (core, nav), pytest tools/tests sim/tests ros/sim_perception/test, the nav_sim_lite closed loop with a fake world, 20 mutation tests of cand_obj

Ships and runs live

  • core/: the mappers, fed only detections, disparity, pose and phase
  • nav/: the navigator, fed the map, pose, clearance, field.yaml and corridor_nav.cfg
  • ros/treemapper_ros, ros/corridor_nav_ros, ros/sim_perception
  • The simulator exists only to produce sensor data and to measure the spray. The mission driver sees only the navigator's status.

Known gaps

  • Nothing is measured on the Orin Nano. Every timing is x86.
  • The recorded Isaac mission and the held-out suite were used for tuning (D60), so they validate and do not test. Fresh seeds 1000 to 1009 are recommended there.
  • The 5x5 recordings from the old machine live only on the external work drive, which is not in git and is not currently mounted on this machine.
  • The rock clutter in rows_8x12.json is requested but not implemented in the simulator.

Terms

A1, A2, A3
Acceptance rows of the miss-reduction work. A1: no lock or dose height more than 1.5 m above the true top. A2: the too-low side does not get worse. A3: on 5x5, far trees have a mean centre error at dose of at most 0.5 m with at most 15 % beyond 0.8 m. A4: at most 12 closed-loop misses (from 35). A5: safety rows pass.
cand_obj
The shipped object-centric mapper. cand_grid is the map-centric alternative, simple the baseline.
corridor
A strip across the row direction. The navigator scans one out, locks its trees, sprays them, then moves on.
field_first
The strategy that scans every corridor, locks once, then sprays. Kept to measure what corridor mode saves.
crown, CHM
A crown is one watershed basin of the canopy height model, a grid of heights over a 0.4 m world lattice.
latched cell
A cell that stayed canopy for 5 weighted frames at 75 % or more. It never un-latches.
hull
The published outline: the convex hull of the latched cells.
lock
The moment the tree list is fixed. In corridor mode that is the navigator's PLANNING phase. A lock stays until the next SCAN.
dose
One SPRAYING interval with a single target id.
hold
Two things. The post-spray hold keeps non-target trees frozen until the drone is 2 m from the hover point. The navigator's HOLD state pauses flight on a lost pose, a stale map or a service failure.
glide
Terrain-following flight between trees.
runaway
A published height that keeps rising after the drone has passed a tree, more than 1.5 m above the truth at the lock.
far, near
Entries first published at least, or less than, 5 m across track.
dh, dc
Published height minus the true top, and distance from the published centre to the true centre.
frames.bin
A recording of exactly what the mapper reads (format TMF1), used to replay a mission offline.
late tree
A tree that appears in the map after its corridor was locked. It goes to the cleanup list.
