## Reading the numbers

- **Noise floor.** Run-to-run noise is 0 (deterministic system and data, see `simple_baseline_synthetic.md`); the relevant
  spread is seed-to-seed, hence the paired win/loss counts above. Every geometry metric is won on every seed.
- **Medium is the level that separates candidates**: recall 0.98, precision 0.99, IoU 0.76, 1 swallowed neighbour and
  0 false trees per mission, against 0.50 / 0.53 / 0.37 / 14.6 / 9.6 for the baseline.
- **Implausible-height frames are mostly a generator/scorer artefact, not height errors.** The generator's spray hover is
  at a fixed ~7 m world z (5.3-6.0 m in hard_s00) while several truth tops are 6.2-8.1 m, and the scorer flags any
  published height above the drone anywhere in the map. The flagged cand_obj heights are within +-0.2 m of the truth:
  hard_s00 6.10 / 5.86 / 5.66 / 5.47 m published for truth 6.17 / 5.81 / 5.89 / 5.30 m with the drone at 5.3-6.0 m;
  hard_s02 7.38 vs 7.47 m; hard_s03 7.60 vs 8.13 m and 7.49 vs 7.60 m; medium_s00 7.15 vs 6.97 m with the drone at
  7.0-7.1 m. One genuine error remains: hard_s01 ID 91, 7.55 m published for a 5.18 m tree (+2.4 m, 23 frames). A system
  that under-reports heights scores "safer" on this count; cand_obj's height bias is -0.002 m overall.
- **Latency** is the one metric lost on every seed: cand_obj waits for the crown top (the tree centre) to be inside the
  image before it can sample a height, where the baseline publishes from the first visible slice. 1.2 / 1.8 / 2.2 s at
  cruise speed; the brief asks for reliable single-pass capture, which holds (recall 0.98 at medium in one pass per corridor).
- **Hard** is a stress level (5x real stereo noise, 4.5 m spacing, 60 % merged boxes). cand_obj keeps precision at 0.97
  and false trees at 0, loses recall (0.86) where the saddle between crowns disappears under noise.
