Height rows are n/a on this recording by design (the truth heights are a constant 4.0 m fallback, scorer R13). cand_obj
publishes 4.3-5.8 m for the nine trees (median of the crown-top cell's median point height). That sits between the
in-canopy point median (3.84 m) and the p99 canopy top (5.88 m) measured in `geometry_check.md`; `simple` (p95 of raw
points) gives 4.8-5.7 m and the existing system 5.4 m on average. Whether navigation wants the typical top-cell height
or the extreme top as its descent reference is open (see the design doc, section 6); on synthetic data, where the truth
is the dome top and the surface is smooth, cand_obj's bias is -0.002 m.

Notes:
- The one weakness relative to the existing system is latency (2.2 s vs 1.2 s): cand_obj publishes a tree only once its
  crown top has been 24 px inside the image for three frames with three finite top samples (rule 3 needs a height first).
- Zero neighbour spill and 0.05 spill: the outline is the hull of latched 0.4 m cells (centres), so it stays inside the
  canopy edge cell.
- The sprayed-tree and descent rules held over all 7 hops (0 non-target changes in 563 judged descent frames, 0 sprayed-tree
  changes), including the post-spray climb hold that keeps every non-target frozen until the drone is 2 m from the hover point.
