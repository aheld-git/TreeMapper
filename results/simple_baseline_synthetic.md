## Notes (M2)

- Suite `suite_v1`: `python3 -m treemapper_tools.synth --out /data/work/synthetic/suite_v1 --levels easy,medium,hard --seeds 0-4`,
  15 missions of 105-142 s, 13-25 trees each, 438 MB, 2 min 40 s on 6 processes. Levels and their calibration:
  `synthetic_calibration.md`. Scored with the fixed scorer (commit e079bff). Truth heights vary, so height
  metrics are valid here, unlike on the Isaac recording.
- **Noise floor.**
  - Run-to-run: every dataset was run twice (`--repeat 2`) and **every headline metric was identical (max diff 0)**.
    The C++ system and the generator are both deterministic (same seed -> byte-identical files, pinned by
    `tests/test_frames_synth.py`). So the run-to-run floor is 0 and it is not the relevant noise.
  - The relevant noise is **seed-to-seed** (column "std over datasets", and the per-group tables). Final recall on
    medium spans 0.30-0.67 over 5 seeds. Compare candidates **paired by seed**, never on unpaired means of 5.
- **What the baseline shows.**
  - Easy (gaps ~1.3 m): it mostly works. Recall 0.90, precision 0.89, IoU 0.58, 0 safety violations.
  - Medium (touching canopies, 3x real stereo noise): it collapses. Recall 0.50, 15 neighbours swallowed per mission, 10 false trees.
  - Hard: recall 0.27. 134 implausible-height frames per mission on average: heights up to 8.4 m from 5x-real
    disparity noise, published above the drone while it descends.
  - Grow-only convex hulls of raw observations plus centroid-containment merging cannot cope with touching trees,
    merged boxes or noisy depth. Spill 0.43 / 0.64 / 0.83 by level.
- **Rules held.**
  - Zero on every level and every seed: undefined heights, new trees after scan, non-target descent changes,
    sprayed-tree changes, new trees inside sprayed trees, vanished or locked IDs.
  - Two defects found and fixed on the way, and these numbers are after both fixes:
    1. A baseline bug: an entry created in SCAN could reach 3 observations in PLANNING and get published while
       locked. Unconfirmed entries are now frozen in locked phases (D23, with a test).
    2. A replay-cadence artefact: a tree published by the last SCAN frame first showed up in the next 5 Hz
       snapshot, already inside PLANNING. The runner now also writes a snapshot at the last frame of every
       phase (D28).
  - **Not clean:** hard has 132 implausible-height frames per mission (worst 225): noise-driven heights above the
    drone during descents. Medium has 1 ID switch after lock (1 seed).
- **Spraying.** On average only 2.9 truth trees get sprayed per mission, out of 4-6 planned. The emulator
  resolves a target only when its descent point lies inside a published outline or within 3 m of a centre. A target
  inside a merged blob resolves to the blob, which is then counted as one sprayed tree, or resolves to nothing.
