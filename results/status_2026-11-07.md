# TreeMapper status, 2026-11-07

Branch `spec/corridor-spray-nav`, pushed to `origin` (`git@github.com:aheld-git/TreeMapper.git`) together with `main` and
`fix/corridor-nav-review`. Written after moving to a new machine. Full detail of the fewer-missed-trees work is in
`results/miss_reduction.md`.

## Done

- **Navigator retry:** a tree skipped for an impossible height gets one more try at the end of the mission if its
  height has become plausible. A retry is dropped, and counted, if it sits within 1.5 m of an already-sprayed tree.
- **Replay recording:** `--record-frames` records only the depth pixels TreeMapper reads (about 120-400 MB per run). A
  replay rebuilds the live map to within 0.0004 m.
- **Centre fix (D66):** mean centre error at spray time for trees first seen far to the side went from about 1.0 m to
  0.1 m on the 5x5 orchard (target 0.5 m). A centre may sit at most 1.0 m outside its tree's outline.
- **Post-spray freeze fix (D67):** non-target trees stay frozen until the drone has left the spray spot.
- **Regression check:** synthetic suite, held-back scenes, the old Isaac recording and the real-flight log all pass.
  Switching the centre fix off restores the old maps byte for byte.

## Open decisions

1. **Height fix (parked):** heights still run up to 3.6 m too high; about 6 of the old 35 misses came from that. The
   diagnosis and best attempt are in `results/miss_reduction/height_experiments.patch`.
2. **Navigator replanning when a tree's centre moves:** on the 5x5 corridor recording, 2 of 13 far trees are still
   beyond 0.8 m at the start of the dosing leg, one over the target. Not built; the live re-run may miss the misses
   target without it.
3. **Double-spray guard distance:** 1.5 m now. 1.3 m (the navigator's reach) still catches every duplicate seen so far
   and drops 2 real neighbouring trees' retries instead of 5.

## Environment on the new machine

- **Isaac 5.1 crashes** at startup on the RTX 5080 (segfault in the RTX renderer plugin). The sim runs on Isaac 6.2,
  which the `TreeHoppingNavigation` repo's `feat/isaac-6.2` branch provides (the `treehoppingnavigation-treehoppingsim`
  image is built from it).
- **Library path:** 6.2 moved the ROS 2 Humble libraries from `isaacsim.ros2.bridge` to `isaacsim.ros2.core`.
  `sim/isaac/run_orchard_sim.sh` now picks whichever exists. This was the only change needed to get missions flying.
- **Images rebuilt:** `treehoppingnavigation-bridge`, `tm_sim_ros`, `treehoppingnavigation-treehoppingsim`.
- **Work drive:** `/data/work` in the containers is the external drive `/media/alex/waspsim/cloud_build_bundle/work`.
  It holds the old Isaac recordings (`mr_frames_*`, `cmp16c_*`) and the detector model. It has only about 11 GB free
