## Notes (M2)

- Scored with the fixed scorer (commit e079bff, review fixes R1-R13). Both columns were scored in the same run.
  `existing` is the recorded `/validation/fusion_snapshot` map. `simple` is `treemapper_replay --system simple`
  on `frames.bin`, the input contract streamed from the bag by `bag_frames.py`, run with the camera chain
  verified in `geometry_check.md`.
- **Height rows are n/a.** The scorer flags the Isaac truth heights as unreliable (a constant 4.0 m fallback, R13;
  see `geometry_check.md`). The simple baseline publishes 4.8-5.7 m (p95 of canopy-point world z, averaged over
  observations). That matches the measured canopy tops of 5.4-5.9 m (p90-p99 of in-canopy points).
- **Hop targets.** The recording's `/spray_target` IDs belong to the existing system. The replay's navigation
  emulator resolves each target to the simple map's own ID through the recorded descent point: the published
  outline containing it, else the nearest centre within 3 m. When SPRAYING ends, the emulator marks that ID
  sprayed. The resolved target timeline (`targets.json`) is what the scorer judges descent safety against.
  So the simple baseline flies the existing system's hop plan. That plan is fixed by the recording and is not
  re-planned on the simple map.
- **Remaining errors.** Truth trees 0 and 1 (gap ~1 m) became one entry, ID 0. Its centre sits between them
  (2.5 m from tree 1), and it was sprayed there. The grow-only hull fusion plus centroid-containment merge have
  no defence against this. Scan latency (2.4 s mean) is about twice the existing system's (1.2 s): an entry is
  published after 3 fused observations.
- **Cost.** Per processed frame on the x86 workstation: 0.50 ms extract (back-projecting the stride-4 grid,
  16000 points) and 0.004 ms integrate. Expect several times this on the Orin (not measured).
- **Drop counters** for the whole mission (rule 7; `counters.json` in the run directory):
  - observations refused: 4157 on sprayed trees, 1626 on frozen non-targets, 467 new-tree attempts in a locked phase;
  - 3.9 M of 24.2 M box points rejected as ground;
  - 35 frames with boxes but no disparity;
  - 614 sleeping frames.
